# Bid v3 Max — `max(DMC, ISDD)` reward signal

**Weights:** `models/bid_v3_max_20M/bid_nn_final.bin`

Same architecture as [bid_v2](bid_v2.md) (Dueling MLP 108→512³→43, ~607K params), trained on a different reward signal: instead of pure DD oracle points, the target is `max(DMC_real_pts, ISDD_real_pts)` — the highest of two realistic play estimates.

See [../reward_studies/v3_max_signal_results.md](../reward_studies/v3_max_signal_results.md) for the full study and motivation.

## Why max?

Both DMC and IS-DD enrichments achieve **~96% of DD points** on average (3.0 pts below DD), but they make different errors. On 4.8M matched samples:

- DMC and IS-DD individually reach contracts ≥120 in **~64%** of cases when DD says it's makeable
- **max(DMC, IS-DD)** reaches contracts ≥120 in **79.5%** of cases
- For contracts ≥140: 43% (DMC) / 47% (IS-DD) → **62%** with max

Training on max gives the bid model a more optimistic but **achievable** signal — the contract is reachable in 80% of attempts, not 64%. This pushes the model to bid more aggressively where the data supports it.

## Training

```bash
cargo run -p colver-core --bin train_bid_nn --features dmc_train --release -- \
    --hidden 512 --layers 3 \
    --num-envs 64 --batch-size 512 \
    --steps 20000000 \
    --pool-file data/pools/dd_1.2M_max_dmc_isdd.bin \
    --reward real \
    --save-dir models/bid_v3_max_20M \
    --eval-freq 1000000 --save-freq 2000000
```

Pool `dd_1.2M_max_dmc_isdd.bin` is built by merging:
- `dd_5M_seq_enriched_dmc.bin` (DMC enrichment, sequential offset 0-5M)
- 12 batches of `tmp_isdd_batchN.bin` (IS-DD enrichment with hard constraints, 1.2M deals)

For each matched deal × 4 suits, `real_pts[s] = max(dmc_pts[s], isdd_pts[s])`.

## Arena results (2000 matches, ±1.1pp std)

| Eval | bid_v3_max_20M | nn_v2 | Margin |
|------|----------------|-------|--------|
| vs `nn_v2_dmc50` (DMC play) | **49.9%** | 50.1% | +11 |
| vs `nn_v2_isdd_no_belief` (IS-DD play) | **50.8%** | 49.2% | +37 |

**Statistically at parity** with nn_v2 in DMC eval, **marginally better** in IS-DD eval (1.5σ above 50%).

This is the **only signal in the v3 study that doesn't lose against nn_v2 in either eval**. Other signals overfit to whichever play model produced the "real" data:
- `exp_e_curriculum` wins +97 in DMC but loses -70 in IS-DD
- `exp_a_dd` (pure DD, 5M steps) is at parity in IS-DD but loses -81 in DMC

## Synergy: bid_v3_max multiplies IS-DD, not DMC

A 4-way round-robin of `{bid_v2, bid_v3_max} × {DouDou50, IS-DD}` (400 matches per pair, no belief net to isolate bidder effect) revealed a sharp asymmetry.

| Play method | bid swap (v2 → v3_max) | Δ win rate |
|---|---|---|
| DMC (DouDou50) | 48.4% → 46.9% | **−1.5%** (null/noise) |
| IS-DD (no belief) | 49.4% → 55.3% | **+5.9%** |

Full matrix (row win rate vs column):

|  | v2+DMC | v2+ISDD | v3_max+DMC | v3_max+ISDD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2+DMC | — | 52.75% | 49.9% | 42.5% |
| v2+ISDD | 47.25% | — | 51.75% | 49.2% |
| v3_max+DMC | 50.1% | 48.25% | — | 42.5% |
| **v3_max+ISDD** | **57.5%** | 50.8% | **57.5%** | — |

**Ranking:** `v3_max+ISDD (55.3%) > v2+ISDD (49.4%) > v2+DMC (48.4%) > v3_max+DMC (46.9%)`.

**Why:** bid_v3_max picks contracts that are *realistically achievable* (~80% of ≥120 attempts succeed vs ~63% for bid_v2's DD-optimized choices). DMC play is too noisy to cash in the extra ~15% of realizable contracts — it needs near-optimal play to convert them. IS-DD, playing close to DD values under belief-sampled worlds, *does* cash them in, so the entire bid_v3_max advantage flows to IS-DD pairings.

**Implication:** don't pair bid_v3_max with DMC play and expect gains. The winning pairing is `bid_v3_max + IS-DD`, and the next obvious upgrade is adding a belief net to it (currently `bid_v3_max_20M_isdd` has none, unlike the #1 leaderboard `nn_v2_isdd`).

## Caveats

- The 200-match initial reports suggested a stronger advantage (+66 / +95) than the 2000-match confirmations show. Always run ≥1000 matches for tournament conclusions.
- The model trains on data generated by DMC and IS-DD, which themselves vary by configuration (residual flag, hard constraints, time budget). Re-run pools if play models change significantly.
